I'm Ömer, a software engineer in Istanbul. For most of the last ten years that has meant mobile engineering: Android and iOS natively, React Native and Flutter when they fit, and the odd bit of web, on whatever a product actually needed.

These days I build and publish my own apps, and I'm open to a new role or to starting something new. If either sounds interesting, <BookCall>book a call</BookCall> or take a look at my <ResumeLink>resume</ResumeLink>.

In 2023 I co-founded [Wite](https://wite.com.tr). We built apps for clients, like <Chip project="mass" />, which puts every electricity distributor in Türkiye behind one app. We built our own products too: <Chip project="nami" />, an AI assistant for iPhone, and <Chip project="suppa" />, a platform for AI chatbots, where I led development and built the mobile SDKs. I also led <Chip project="clarvia" />, an R&D project on a voice assistant that answers an electricity company's outage calls.

Alongside it, until late 2025, I was a contractor through [RE Partners](https://www.re-partners.com) on Android teams abroad: <Chip project="gymshark-training" /> and <Chip project="gymshark-shop" />, then <Chip project="qoluna" />, a health app that reads your wearables.

Before that I spent almost three years at [Mobillium](https://www.mobillium.com), where I came to lead more than twenty Android developers. I led the Android app of <Chip project="tom-bank-hadi" />, built from the ground up for what is now a digital bank, and built <Chip project="pawsapp" /> for a team in London. We also kept busy apps like <Chip project="migros" /> and <Chip project="nefis-yemek-tarifleri" /> running, and wrote the internal SDKs the other teams built on.

Earlier still, at [Ketchup](https://www.ketchuployalty.com) I worked on apps like <Chip project="tikla-gelsin" />, moved code from Java to Kotlin and worked on payments. At Bulut Yazılım I built an advertising SDK for the tablets in the back of <Chip project="taxi-7x24" /> cabs. And in 2016 I started out at [Ovidos](https://ovidos.com), writing Swift for iOS apps like <Chip project="coiny" /> while I was still studying computer engineering.

Outside client work, my main project is <Chip project="vakitler" />, a prayer times app for Android that I started in 2024 and still maintain. I also build open-source things like <Chip project="moodstone" /> and <Chip project="beetle" /> on [GitHub](https://github.com/karacca), and I [write](/writing) about what I learn. In 2026 I taught an [online Android course](/writing/advanced-android-from-code-to-production) built around one app, and since 2022 I've interviewed developers for [Lemon.io](https://lemon.io).

I live with my wife and our daughter, and in my free time I play chess, swim and read history.
