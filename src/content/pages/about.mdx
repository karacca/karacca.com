I'm Ömer, an Android developer based in <Chip icon="pin">Istanbul</Chip>. I work at <Chip icon="wite" href="https://wite.com.tr">Wite</Chip>, and most of what I make runs on <Chip icon="android">Android</Chip>, written in <Chip icon="kotlin">Kotlin</Chip> and <Chip icon="compose">Jetpack Compose</Chip>.

Outside of work I build things in the open on <Chip icon="github" href="https://github.com/karacca">GitHub</Chip>. One of them is <Chip icon="nami" href="/projects/nami">Nami</Chip>, a unified AI chat app. It is also the app at the heart of a course I'm writing, Advanced Android: From Code to Production, which uses it to teach production-grade Android development.

The most recent one is <Chip icon="moodstone" href="/projects/moodstone">Moodstone</Chip>, a set of animated, procedurally generated agent avatars for React Native, and a small step outside Android. Further back there is <Chip icon="beetle" href="/projects/beetle">Beetle</Chip>, a library that collects feedback and bug reports from Android apps into GitHub Issues, along with a few smaller experiments like a note-taking app and a gradient button.

This site is where I keep the rest: [writing](/writing) about what I learn, the [projects](/projects) I've worked on, [bookmarks](/bookmarks) worth keeping, and the [desk](/desk) I work from.
