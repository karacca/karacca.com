Hi! I'm Ömer, a software engineer in Istanbul. Although I don't believe in sharp lines in software development, most people know me for my mobile apps. My journey started a decade ago, when everyone was walking around with their phones out, catching Pokémon. Ten years on, I've built apps, led teams and co-founded a company, and I've spent a good part of that time helping other engineers grow through mentoring and teaching. I still love the craft of writing software as much as I did then.

In 2023 I co-founded [Wite](https://wite.com.tr), where I led projects end to end and mentored the engineers in our team of twelve. Among them were <Chip project="mass" />, which puts every electricity distributor in Türkiye behind one app, and <Chip project="vesika" />, an AI assistant for lawyers that finds case law and drafts petitions. Customer support was another thread: I led <Chip project="suppa" />, a no-code platform for support chatbots, built its mobile SDKs, and ran an R&D project on a voice assistant that answers an electricity company's outage calls.

We also made consumer apps of our own. Most recently I built <Chip project="kilowatt" /> from the app to its backend and website, putting every EV charging station in Türkiye on one map. I started <Chip project="nami" />, an AI assistant for iPhone with the major models under one subscription, and made the first version of <Chip project="owlio" />, an app for Türkiye's English exams, before leading its rebuild.

From late 2022 to late 2025 I was a contractor through [RE Partners](https://www.re-partners.com) on Android teams abroad. After six months on <Chip project="gymshark-training" />, I spent a year and a half on <Chip project="gymshark-shop" />, working with a large team spread across time zones on a codebase of about a hundred modules. Then I moved to <Chip project="qoluna" />, a health app that reads your wearables.

Since 2022 I've also been working remotely through [Lemon.io](https://lemon.io), on client projects as a senior Android engineer, and interviewing the senior and lead Android developers who apply to join it.

In 2020 I joined [Mobillium](https://www.mobillium.com), and over almost three years I came to lead more than twenty Android developers. I interviewed candidates, ran performance reviews, mentored the team and gave tech talks, and I wrote the internal SDKs the other teams built on. I led the Android app of <Chip project="tom-bank-hadi" />, built from the ground up for what is now a digital bank, and shipped <Chip project="pawsapp" /> for a team in London, while we kept busy apps like <Chip project="migros" /> and <Chip project="nefis-yemek-tarifleri" /> running.

In 2019, at [Ketchup](https://www.ketchuployalty.com), I rewrote the <Chip project="pinar-su" /> Android app in Kotlin and added in-app card payments, which Pınar advertised as a first in Türkiye. I also kept apps like <Chip project="tikla-gelsin" /> and <Chip project="volvo-car-prime" /> running on Android and iOS.

At Bulut Yazılım in 2018, I designed and built an advertising SDK for Android that showed targeted ads on the tablets in the back of <Chip project="taxi-7x24" /> cabs, and added features to <Chip project="pepapp" />, a period tracker.

I started out at [Ovidos](https://ovidos.com) in the summer of 2016, writing Swift while I was still studying computer engineering. I developed the iPhone app of <Chip project="coiny" />, a crypto wallet, and worked on the courier app of <Chip project="pakettaxi" />.

On the side, my main project is <Chip project="vakitler" />, a prayer times app for Android that I started in 2024 and still maintain. I also build open-source things like <Chip project="moodstone" /> and <Chip project="beetle" /> on [GitHub](https://github.com/karacca), and I share what I learn: I [write](/writing), give talks and teach courses, among them an [Android course](/writing/advanced-android-from-code-to-production) built around one app.

I live with my wife and our daughter, and in my free time I play chess, swim and read history.
