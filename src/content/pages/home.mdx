I'm Ömer. For ten years I've built apps for everyday things: paying with <Chip project="tom-bank-hadi" />, shopping with <Chip project="gymshark-shop" />, checking power outages with <Chip project="mass" />, finding a charger with <Chip project="kilowatt" /> and keeping prayer times with <Chip project="vakitler" />, an app of my own.

I started out in 2016, while still studying computer engineering. At [Mobillium](https://www.mobillium.com) I came to lead more than twenty Android developers. I worked on Android teams abroad through [RE Partners](https://www.re-partners.com), and in 2023 I co-founded [Wite](https://wite.com.tr), where I led projects end to end.

Since 2022 I've also been interviewing senior and lead Android developers for [Lemon.io](https://lemon.io), and I share what I learn in writing, talks and courses. There's more [about me](/about).
