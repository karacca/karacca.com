Pınar Su is the bottled water of Pınar, the food and drink brand of İzmir's Yaşar Group. Its app launched in 2013 as Pınar Su Sipariş and is now called Yaşam Pınarım, after the name on its bottles.

## What I did

At Ketchup I maintained and improved the Pınar Su apps for Android and iOS, working on their performance, their stability and how they felt to use.

## The app

In 2019 an order took three steps: pick products from the price list for your area, set how many, then choose a delivery time and pay at the door.

## Since then

The app now repeats a past order in one tap, and since September 2026 orders earn loyalty points.
