Vakitler takes its times from Diyanet, Türkiye's Presidency of Religious Affairs. It is free, has no ads, and works in Turkish and English.

## What I did

Vakitler is my own project. I started it in December 2024, and I still build and maintain it on my own.

- **1.0**, December 2024: prayer times for a place picked from a list.
- **2.0**, February 2026: automatic location, the iftar countdown, the Hijri date and a more reliable widget.
- **3.0**, August 2026: reminders, several saved locations, light and dark themes and new widgets.

## The app

The home screen answers one question: how long until the next prayer. A large countdown sits in the middle with the six times of the day below it, and the whole screen takes the color of the next prayer, from blue at Fajr to indigo at Isha. During Ramadan a second counter runs until iftar.

- **Reminders.** For each prayer, choose whether and how early, from two hours before to the moment it begins. I wrote about [why the minutes are worked out when the notification shows](/writing/never-trust-the-delivery-time).
- **Locations.** Follow the device, or save places from a list and switch between them from the home screen.
- **Qibla.** A small compass on the home screen shows which way to turn.
- **Widgets.** The day's times and the countdown on the home screen.
