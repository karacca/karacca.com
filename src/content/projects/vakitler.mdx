Vakitler is a prayer times app for Android. It shows the day's times from Diyanet, Turkey's Presidency of Religious Affairs, counts down to the next prayer, finds the qibla, and reminds you before the prayers you choose. It is free, has no ads, and works in Turkish and English.

I started it at Wite in December 2024 and still maintain it.

## First launch

Onboarding takes four pages, one feature each: the day's times, the countdown, location and reminders. Location can come from the device or from a list, and reminders can wait until later.

## Home screen

The home screen answers one question: how long until the next prayer. A large countdown sits in the middle, with the Hijri date above it and, during Ramadan, a second counter until iftar. Below are the six times of the day. Past ones fade, the next one is outlined, and the whole screen takes that prayer's color, from blue at Fajr to indigo at Isha.

Each row has an alarm toggle, so a reminder is one tap away. The location chip at the top switches between saved places, and the small dial beside it is a qibla compass whose arrow shows which way to turn.

## Reminders

You pick which prayers to be reminded of and how early, separately for each one, from two hours before to the moment the time begins. Reminders are exact alarms. The minutes in the notification are worked out when it is shown, not when it was scheduled, because some phones deliver alarms late. I wrote about [how a Xiaomi phone sat on one for an hour](/writing/never-trust-the-delivery-time).

## Locations

Vakitler can follow the device location and update the times when you travel. You can also pick a country, city and district from the list, save several places, and switch between them from the home screen.

## Widgets

Home screen widgets show the day's times and the countdown to the next prayer. They are built with Glance and update without waking the device.

## How it is built

The app is written in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose, split into data, domain and presentation layers. Every screen has screenshot tests in light and dark, Turkish and English, portrait and landscape, with the date and clock pinned so the images never drift. Apart from the store set at the top, the screenshots on this page are those test images.

## Releases

- **1.0**, December 2024: prayer times for a place picked from the list.
- **2.0**, February 2026: automatic location, the iftar countdown, the Hijri date and a more reliable widget.
- **3.0**, August 2026: reminders, multiple saved locations, light and dark themes, in-app language and new widgets.
- **3.1**, September 2026: reminders right when a prayer begins.
