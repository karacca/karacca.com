Migros is the app of Migros, one of Türkiye's largest supermarket chains. Sanal Market delivers groceries on the day and time you pick, Migros Hemen brings them in minutes, and Migros Yemek brings meals from restaurants. It has more than 10 million downloads on Google Play.

## What I did

At Mobillium I worked on the Migros Android app, building new features and keeping a large app stable.

## One app

While I was at Mobillium, Migros brought its separate apps together:

- **May 2021.** Migros Hemen, until then an app of its own, moved into the Migros app.
- **June 2021.** Sanal Market, Hemen, Migros Ekstra and the MoneyPay wallet came together in one app, with bigger product photos and easier search. Migros called it a step toward a super app.
- **June 2022.** Migros Yemek arrived as a new tab, starting in Ataşehir, Istanbul.

## The app today

- **Sanal Market.** More than 40,000 products, delivered in refrigerated vans on the day and time you pick, up to six days ahead, or collected from a Migros store.
- **Migros Hemen.** Thousands of products in minutes, with the courier on a map.
- **Migros Yemek.** Meals from local restaurants, in some regions so far.
- **More stores.** Migros Ekstra for electronics, toys and home goods, Macroonline from Macrocenter, Mion for personal care, Tazedirekt for produce from farms, and Petimo for pets.
- **Payments.** Cards, MoneyPay, BKM Express and Garanti Pay, along with Money Pro cards, digital shopping codes and coupons. Products can be returned at the door when an order arrives.
