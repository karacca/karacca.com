TOM Bank is a digital participation bank in Türkiye. Its main shareholder is also behind A101, the discount supermarket chain, so the app is built around those stores: you pay at the till with it, take out or put in cash at any A101, and shop now and pay later.

## What I did

At Mobillium I led Android development for Hadi. We built the app from the ground up for TOM, with a team of Android developers.

- **Foundation.** I set up the project and its architecture, so a team that size could build features side by side.
- **Security.** As a financial app, it had to be secure and ready to scale from the first release, and that shaped most of our choices.
- **Team.** I guided the team's technical decisions and reviewed their code.

Hadi launched at the end of 2022, and I left Mobillium shortly after, in January 2023.

## The app

In its first months, Hadi was an e-money app from TOM Digital, not yet a bank:

- **Hadi Mastercard.** A free prepaid card that works anywhere, topped up from the app.
- **A101.** Pay at the till with a QR code, and take out cash for free at any A101.
- **Hadi Gold.** A membership with cash back on every A101 purchase.
- **Hadi Veresiye.** Named after buying on tab at the corner shop: A101 groceries now, paid a month later with no interest.
- **Transfers.** Free money transfers around the clock, and bill payments.

## Since then

TOM Bank opened Hadi to everyone as its digital branch in March 2024. The app has since added a credit card, accounts that earn a share of the bank's profit, gold and currency, and shop now and pay later at more stores.
