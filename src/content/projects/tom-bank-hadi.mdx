TOM Bank Hadi is the app of TOM Bank, a digital participation bank in Türkiye. Its main shareholder, Turgut Aydın Holding, is also behind A101, the discount supermarket chain, and the app is built around those stores: you pay at the till with it, take out or put in cash at any A101, and shop now and pay later. It has more than 10 million downloads on Google Play.

## What I did

At Mobillium I led Android development for Hadi. We built the app from the ground up for TOM, and I set up its architecture with the security and scale of a financial app in mind. Hadi launched at the end of 2022, and I left Mobillium shortly after, in January 2023.

## Hadi at launch

In its first months, Hadi was an e-money app from TOM Digital, not yet a bank:

- **Hadi Mastercard.** A free prepaid card that works anywhere, online and contactless, topped up from the app.
- **A101.** Pay at the till by scanning a QR code, and take out cash for free at any A101 till.
- **Hadi Gold.** A membership with 3% cash back on every A101 purchase and 10% on fruit and vegetables.
- **Hadi Veresiye.** Named after buying on tab at the corner shop: A101 groceries now, paid a month later with no interest. Installment credit for TVs and small appliances followed.
- **Transfers.** Free money transfers around the clock, and bill payments.

## TOM Bank Hadi today

TOM Bank opened Hadi to everyone as its digital branch in March 2024, and the app has grown well past what we launched:

- **Hadi Black.** A paid credit card with cash back at markets, restaurants and fuel stations.
- **Veresiye and installments.** Buy now and pay two months later at A101, English Home, Eve and other markets, or spread a purchase over up to 24 months.
- **Accounts.** An account that earns every day, and term accounts in lira, dollars and euros. As a participation bank, TOM pays a share of its profit instead of interest.
- **Gold and currency.** Buy and sell dollars, euros, gold and silver, and save gold bit by bit.
- **Transfers and bills.** Free transfers around the clock, to contacts without an IBAN, cash in and out at any A101, and automatic bill payments.
