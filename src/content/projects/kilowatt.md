Türkiye has more than 180 charging operators, each with its own app and its own map. Kilowatt brings all of their stations together, so one app is enough wherever you charge.

## What I did

I started Kilowatt at Wite in May 2026 and built it on my own: the iOS and Android app, the backend and the website. It reached the App Store and Google Play in June. The stations come from EPDK's public registry of licensed charging stations, and the prices from each operator's own tariffs.

## The app

- **Map.** Every station, marked with its operator's logo, filtered by socket type, operator or your favorites.
- **Prices.** Each operator's price per kWh for AC, DC and HPC.
- **Stations.** The address, every socket with its type and power, photos and reviews, and directions in Apple or Google Maps.
- **Reviews.** Anyone signed in can rate a station, write a review or add a photo.

The app is in Turkish.
