Qoluna Health is a health app from the founders of RE Partners. It gathers data from wearables and health apps, learns what is normal for you, and reads your trends against peer-reviewed studies to explain what is changing in your body and what small steps to take next. It is meant to support conversations with a doctor, not to replace them.

## What I did

I moved to Qoluna in late 2024, through RE Partners, after a year and a half on [Gymshark Shop](/projects/gymshark-shop), and worked on its Android app until I left in November 2025. The app reached the App Store in March 2026 and Google Play in September 2026.

## The app

- **Integrations.** Data from wearables and health apps, such as Apple Watch, Fitbit and Oura, gathered into one long-term record.
- **Quality of life model.** A Body Action Score for sleep, recovery and activity, and a Wellness Index from a short check-in on stress, focus and mood.
- **Insights.** Heart, sleep, activity and breathing, each with observations an AI agent draws from your data and medical research.
- **Action steps.** Small goals that fit your trends, like a few aerobic sessions a week or a breathing practice before bed.
- **Analytics.** Charts of each metric over time, against your personal baseline.
- **MD report.** A summary of your data to download and take to your doctor.
