Qoluna gathers data from wearables and health apps, learns what is normal for you, and explains what is changing and what small steps to take next. It is meant to support conversations with a doctor, not to replace them.

## What I did

I moved to Qoluna through RE Partners in late 2024, after a year and a half on [Gymshark Shop](/projects/gymshark-shop), and worked on its Android app until I left in November 2025. The app reached the App Store in March 2026 and Google Play in September 2026.

## The app

- **Integrations.** Data from devices like Apple Watch, Fitbit and Oura, gathered into one long-term record.
- **Scores.** A score for sleep, recovery and activity, and a wellness index from a short check-in on stress, focus and mood.
- **Insights.** Heart, sleep, activity and breathing, each read against your own baseline and medical research.
- **Doctor report.** A summary of your data to take to your doctor.
