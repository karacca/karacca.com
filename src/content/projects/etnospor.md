The World Ethnosport Confederation, based in Istanbul, works to keep traditional sports and games alive. At its Ethnosport Culture Festival the app turns into a game: visitors scan QR codes around the grounds to earn points.

## What I did

I built the Android app in the summer of 2021, while another developer built the iOS app, and both reached the stores that November. From 2022 I looked after both apps.

- **2024.** At Wite, the 6th festival's look, a new home screen and the leaderboard.
- **2025.** The 7th festival's look and a fresh leaderboard.

## The app

- **Sports.** Every traditional sport with its story, photos and upcoming events.
- **Calendar.** The year's events, each with its time, its place on a map and a button to join.
- **Announcements.** News from the confederation.
- **Points.** Sign in with a phone number and scan the codes at the festival. Staff can look a visitor up and add or spend their points.
- **Leaderboard.** The visitors with the most points.

The app is in Turkish.
