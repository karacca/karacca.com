Volvo Car Prime is the loyalty program of Volvo Car Türkiye, and this is its app, for everyone who drives a Volvo in Türkiye. Each car gets a segment from its visits to authorized services over the last year, and the perks grow with it: discounts on maintenance and parts, roadside assistance and towing, a replacement car, valet service, and a discount on the next Volvo. Partner brands add offers for fashion, travel, sport and the arts. The program started in June 2017.

## What I did

At Ketchup I maintained the Volvo Car Prime apps for Android and iOS, keeping them stable and running smoothly for Volvo owners.

## The app today

In May 2020, a few weeks after I left Ketchup, Volvo relaunched the program with a renewed app. Today it has:

- **Benim Volvom.** Your car's model, segment, plate, chassis number, and its last service date and mileage.
- **Privileges.** Offers from partner brands, sorted into fashion, comfort, travel, sport, hobbies and the arts.
- **Dealers and services.** The nearest ones, with buttons to call, get directions or book a service appointment.
- **Help.** Volvo Car Asistans for roadside help, and suggestions or complaints sent from the app.
- **Volvo.** News, events and the model range.
