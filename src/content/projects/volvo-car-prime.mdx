Volvo Car Prime is the loyalty program of Volvo Car Türkiye. The more often a car visits an authorized service, the more its owner gets: discounts on maintenance and parts, roadside help, a replacement car, and offers from partner brands.

## What I did

At Ketchup I maintained the Volvo Car Prime apps for Android and iOS, keeping them stable and running smoothly for Volvo owners. In May 2020, a few weeks after I left, Volvo relaunched the program with a renewed app.
