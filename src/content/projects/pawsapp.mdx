PawsApp is a dog walking service in Central London, with one app for owners and another for the walkers, whom it calls carers.

## What I did

At Mobillium I built PawsApp from zero for its London team: the app for dog owners and the app for walkers, with booking in real time, scheduling, profiles and payments. Both apps reached the App Store in November 2021.

## The app

- **Walks.** A walk within the hour, a scheduled walk or monthly walks, with a walker who is free.
- **Live walks.** The route on a map with the time and distance so far, and the owner can reach the walker from the same screen.
- **Report card.** How the walk went and a note from the walker, and the owner rates the walker.
- **Carer app.** Walkers go online to get requests nearby, see what a walk pays before they confirm it, and keep their bookings on a calendar.
