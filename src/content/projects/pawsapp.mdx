PawsApp is a dog walking service in Central London. Owners book a walk in the app, a vetted walker from the neighbourhood comes to the door, and they follow the walk live on a map. A report card arrives when the dog is back home. Walkers have an app of their own for taking requests, walking and getting paid. PawsApp says it has tracked more than 50,000 walks.

## What I did

At Mobillium I built PawsApp from zero for its London team: the app for dog owners and the app for walkers, with booking in real time, scheduling, profiles and payments. Both apps reached the App Store in November 2021.

## The app

- **Walks.** An ASAP walk, with a walker at the door within 60 minutes, a scheduled walk, or monthly walks. A free meet and greet with a local walker can come first.
- **Matching.** The app matches a dog with a walker who is free, so there is no back and forth with walkers who are busy.
- **Walkers.** Each walker is interviewed in person, trained online and background-checked. Fewer than one in five applicants gets in, and every walk is insured.
- **Live walks.** The route on a map, the time and distance so far, and a notification when the dog pees or poops. The owner can contact the walker from the same screen.
- **Report card.** After the walk: how long it took, how far they went, whether the dog peed and pooped, and a note from the walker. The owner rates the walker.
- **Payments.** Card payments inside the app.

## The carer app

Walkers, whom the app calls carers, go online to receive walk requests nearby. They see what a walk pays before they confirm it, keep their bookings on a calendar, and report toilet breaks while they walk. Earnings and tips are paid to their bank account.
