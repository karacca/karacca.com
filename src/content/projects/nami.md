Nami gives you the major AI models for one subscription instead of one each. You can talk to models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI and others, switch between them in the middle of a chat, or ask several at once and compare their answers.

## What I did

I started Nami at Wite in October 2025.

- **1.0.** Built in React Native, and released on the App Store in January 2026.
- **2.0.** A new design with sheets for models, chat history and settings, released in April. I built most of it.
- **3.0.** A fresh start, released in October. I wrote the product spec it was built from, screen by screen, and the team built it.

## The app

The App Store screenshots still show Nami before 3.0.

- **Models.** Every model with its cost and the plan it needs. With several picked, each answer gets its own pane.
- **Projects.** Chats grouped with shared files and instructions.
- **Memory.** A few short notes about you that every model gets as context, which you can see and delete.
- **Tools.** Web search, reading web pages, and image and video generation, with one prompt going to up to four video models.
- **Voice.** Dictate a message, or have an answer read aloud.

## Where it stands

Nami is on the App Store for iPhone in seven languages. The Android course I gave, [Advanced Android: From Code to Production](/writing/advanced-android-from-code-to-production), is built around an Android version of it.
