Suppa lets a business build an AI chatbot for customer support without writing code, and put it on its website or in its mobile app. The chatbot answers from the business's own documents and can act on its systems, like checking an order or booking a call.

## What I did

We built Suppa at Wite as our own product, and it launched in August 2024. I led its development and built its mobile SDKs, which put a business's chatbot inside its app with a single call, styled the way it was set up on the web. The [Android SDK](https://github.com/witetech/suppa-android) is open source.

## The platform

- **Knowledgebase.** Documents, web pages, videos or plain text for the chatbot to answer from.
- **Canvas.** A drag-and-drop editor for how a conversation flows.
- **Actions.** The business's own APIs, which the AI calls when a conversation needs them.
- **Install.** One script tag on a website, or an SDK in a mobile app.
