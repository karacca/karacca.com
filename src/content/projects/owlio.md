Owlio prepares you for Türkiye's English exams with real questions from past sessions and practice sets an AI writes around your level. It started at Wite as Quiz and took its name and owl mascot in 2026.

## What I did

- **1.0.** I built the first version in Flutter at the end of 2023, while a colleague wrote the backend. It reached the App Store in April 2024.
- **2.0.** In August 2024 we started over, and I led it with a team of three developers. I set up the project, reviewed the team's work, and built the onboarding, the quiz with its explanations, and sign-in. It shipped at the end of October.

## The app

- **Past exams.** Real questions from past sessions, which you can leave halfway and finish later.
- **AI sets.** Ten questions at a time, picked for your level and your weakest topics.
- **Explanations.** Why the right choice is right, after every answer.
- **Leaderboard and analytics.** Rankings and your success rate by topic, added by the team in 2025.

The app is in Turkish and English.
