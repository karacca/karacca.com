PaketTaxi is a courier company from Istanbul. Two founders who had run restaurants started it in 2016 to take deliveries off the hands of restaurants and online stores. A business called a courier from a web panel, the customer could follow the order live and see who was bringing it, and couriers got around by motorbike, bicycle, inline skates or on foot. By January 2017 it made more than 40,000 deliveries a month with 87 couriers.

## What I did

At Ovidos I worked on PaketTaxi's courier app: the features couriers use during a delivery, location tracking and routes.

## The courier app

- **Orders.** A new order shows the restaurant, the pickup address and when the food will be ready, with a few seconds to accept or reject it.
- **Routes.** A map with live traffic and the way to the restaurant, then to the customer.
- **History.** Past deliveries with their times and how they were paid, and a count for each month.
- **Tracking.** Every delivery is followed by GPS. Later in 2018 the app started carrying orders from online stores too.

## Since then

- **2019.** Migros Hemen launched in February with PaketTaxi couriers. In October Migros bought a 25% stake, and PaketTaxi spread to more cities.
- **2021.** About 20 million packages carried since the start, more than 75,000 a day, with over 3,000 couriers in 69 provinces.
- **2022–2024.** Migros raised its stake to 75%, then to 92%.

Today PaketTaxi says it has 7,000 couriers making 7 million deliveries a month.
