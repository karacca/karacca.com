PaketTaxi was started in Istanbul in 2016 by two former restaurant owners, to take deliveries off the hands of restaurants and online stores. Today it is part of Migros and says it makes 7 million deliveries a month.

## What I did

At Ovidos I worked on PaketTaxi's courier app: the features couriers use during a delivery, location tracking and routes.

## The app

- **Orders.** A new order shows the restaurant, the pickup address and when the food will be ready, with a few seconds to accept or reject it.
- **Routes.** A map with live traffic and the way to the restaurant, then to the customer.
- **History.** Past deliveries with their times and how they were paid.
