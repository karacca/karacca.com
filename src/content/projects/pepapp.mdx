Pepapp is a period tracker made in Istanbul. It predicts your period, PMS, ovulation and fertile days, and its penguin mascot sends tips and kind notifications along the way. Around the calendar there is Pepzine, a magazine on health and relationships, and PepForum, where women ask each other questions without their names. It began on Android in 2015 and came to the iPhone in 2016, and today it has more than a million downloads on Google Play.

## What I did

At Bulut Yazılım I worked on Pepapp in 2018, building new features and making the app better to use.

## Pepapp in 2018

Back then the app had:

- **The assistant.** A home screen with the penguin, the day of your cycle, the days left to ovulation and tips for that phase.
- **The calendar.** Period, ovulation, PMS and fertile days in color, and a log of flow, pain, symptoms, intercourse and notes for each day.
- **Trying to conceive.** Guidance for women who wanted to get pregnant.
- **Pepzine.** Articles inside the app.
- **More.** A passcode lock, backups with Google or Facebook sign-in, and an iMessage sticker pack with the penguin.

## Pepapp today

- **Calendar.** Predictions for period, PMS, ovulation and fertile days, with discharge, mood, symptoms, birth control and notes logged day by day.
- **Cycle report.** A summary of the last six months to share with a doctor.
- **Pregnancy mode.** The home screen switches to weeks and trimesters, with tips for each.
- **PepCards.** Short cards picked for you, from care routines to the menu of the day.
- **Pepzine and PepForum.** The magazine, and a forum for anonymous questions.
- **More.** An app lock, Apple Health, English and Turkish, and a premium tier without ads.
