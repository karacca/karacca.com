Taxi 7x24 was a taxi service in Istanbul, launched with Turkcell in May 2018. It put two tablets in every taxi. The driver's tablet took calls, showed the way and was the taximeter itself, updated over the air. The passenger's tablet, on the back of the front seat, had 4.5G internet, Turkcell's music, TV and magazine apps, news and deals for the ride. Riders called a taxi from the app or from BiP and paid by card, in cash or with Paycell on their phone bill.

## What I did

At Bulut Yazılım I worked on Taxi 7x24's passenger app and its kiosk apps, the ones on the back-seat tablets, building features, fixing bugs and making them faster. I also designed and built an advertising SDK for Android in Kotlin. The kiosk apps used it to show targeted ads during the ride and bring in more revenue.

## The passenger app

- **Calling a taxi.** A map with your location and the taxi stands nearby, and one button to call.
- **Fares.** An estimate before the ride, comparing the shortest, fastest and cheapest routes.
- **Taxi types.** A choice between the yellow, turquoise and black taxis of Istanbul.
- **History.** Past rides with their fares, and ratings for the driver and the car.

## Since then

The passenger app was redesigned in February 2019, as I was leaving Bulut Yazılım, and again in May 2020, its last update. A relaunched Taksi 7x24 put out new apps in 2026.
