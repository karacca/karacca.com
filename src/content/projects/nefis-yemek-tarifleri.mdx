Nefis Yemek Tarifleri is the app of nefisyemektarifleri.com, one of Türkiye's most-read recipe sites, built around a community of home cooks. It began as a blog in 2007. Members send in recipes they have cooked and photographed, and others try them and share photos of how theirs turned out. There are more than 900,000 recipes and thousands of videos, and the app has more than 10 million downloads on Google Play.

## What I did

At Mobillium I worked on the Nefis Yemek Tarifleri Android app, building new features and keeping a high-traffic app easy to maintain.

## The app today

Since then the app has been redesigned. Today it has:

- **Recipes.** Every recipe is tried and photographed, many with a video and a photo for each step, along with comments and photos from people who made it.
- **Search.** Search more than 900,000 recipes, or pick the ingredients you have at home and get recipes that use them. Filters cover what a recipe has or leaves out, category, servings, cooking time, method and equipment.
- **Recipe notebooks.** Save recipes into notebooks of your own, open them from the app or the website, and follow other people's.
- **What should I cook?** A menu picked with care for every day.
- **Profiles.** Write and send your own recipes, follow other cooks, and see what they have sent and tried.
- **More.** Videos, menus for occasions like tea time and birthdays, a blog, lists and calorie counts.
