Tıkla Gelsin, which means "click and it comes", belongs to TAB Gıda, the company behind those restaurant chains in Türkiye. Today's app has more than 5 million downloads on Google Play.

## What I did

At Ketchup I maintained the Tıkla Gelsin apps for Android and iOS, working on new features and bug fixes, and helped bring an older codebase up to date.

## The app

You picked an address, then one of the brands that delivered there, built a menu and placed the order. The app also kept campaigns and coupons, your last orders to repeat, and Para Puan, loyalty points to spend on the next order.

## Since then

In May 2020, a month after I left Ketchup, the app came out in a new red and yellow design. In December 2020 Tıkla Gelsin moved to a new app from Ata Express, the group's delivery company, which added pickup, meal cards and a wallet of its own.
