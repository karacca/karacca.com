Tıkla Gelsin is where you order from Burger King, Popeyes, Arby's, Sbarro, Usta Dönerci and the group's other chains in Türkiye, delivered to your door or ready to pick up. Its name means "click and it comes". Today's app has more than 5 million downloads on Google Play.

## What I did

At Ketchup I maintained the Tıkla Gelsin apps for Android and iOS, built for TAB Gıda, the company behind those chains in Türkiye. I worked on new features and bug fixes, and helped bring an older codebase up to date.

## The app back then

You picked an address, then one of the brands that delivered there: Burger King, Popeyes, Usta Dönerci, Sbarro, Arby's or Amasya Et Ürünleri. From there you built a menu, chose how to pay and placed the order. The app also kept campaigns and coupons, saved baskets, your last ten orders to repeat, live support, and Para Puan, loyalty points you could spend on the next order.

## Since then

- **May 2020.** A month after I left Ketchup, the app came out with a new logo and design, in red and yellow instead of green, along with a map for addresses and contactless delivery.
- **December 2020.** Tıkla Gelsin moved to a new app from Ata Express, the group's delivery company, and the old app later left the stores.

## The app today

- **Sana Gelsin and Gel Al.** Delivery to your door, or order ahead and pick up without waiting in line, with nearby restaurants on a map.
- **Brands.** Burger King, Popeyes, Arby's, Subway, Sbarro, Usta Dönerci, Usta Pideci and more.
- **Campaigns.** Coupons, free size-ups, a student discount and a game with prizes.
- **Payments.** Cards, payment at the door, meal cards like Multinet and Ticket Restoran, and Tıkla Pay, a wallet that earns Tıkla Para.
- **Tracking.** Live order tracking, a chatbot and live support.
